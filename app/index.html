Before you start

  • One playthrough is enough. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no DLC trophies, and free roam carries on after the credits.
  • No mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
  • One possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — do Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (see its guide). Everything else can be done after the story.
  • Cheats switch trophies off. Turning on a cheat disables trophies (and saving), so keep them off a trophy run.
  • About 200 hours to the platinum, most of it on the 100% checklist.
